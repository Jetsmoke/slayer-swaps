# Slayer Swaps

Slayer Swaps is a plugin that aims to help you get to and from your next slayer task as effortlessly as possible. It swaps your left-click on certain items and objects, like fairy rings, teleport items of all types, and much more.

To start, simply right-click your slayer helmet and select your favourite slayer master, followed by your preferred method of teleporting there.

The plugin highlights your chosen method of travel to quickly get you to your next task. After receiving your next task, you can right-click your slayer helmet to choose your preferred location for that task, followed by your chosen method of getting there. These preferences can be changed at any time, and will remain in place the next time you get the same task.

## Features
- **Next step tracking:** always shows where your task is and what to do next.
- **Minimap arrow:** just keep following that arrow
- **Task monsters:** automatically highlight the slayer monster assigned 
- **House teleports:** portal nexus, portal chamber, jewellery box and house fairy ring, with the menu hotkey shown.
- **Wilderness:** obelisks, levers and Wilderness locations, used automatically for Krystilia tasks.
- **No Slayer Helmet?:** black mask and enchanted gem also supported for slayers of all levels

Most methods of travel are supported, but if see anything I've missed or have any other sort of suggestions feel free to reach out.
